# Career-coach eval scenarios (v1)

Human checklist. For each scenario: run with the skill loaded, score pass/fail.
Do not require SkillEvaluator tooling for v1.

**Global graders (almost every scenario):**

- Integrity: no invented metrics, titles, or ownership
- Missing ≠ lacking: unknowns marked/asked, not assumed absent in the person
- Questions: at most a few focused questions; not a questionnaire dump
- Speech: concrete-first; no AI fog, chatbot manners, sycophancy, or brochure tone
- Internal jargon: ADM/modes not lectured unless asked
- Market/domain: no unsolicited universal CV rules or engineering defaults

---

## A — Weak CV achievement

**Setup:** User: “Worked on React applications and developed reusable components.”

**Pass:**

- Treats as responsibility/duty, not a finished achievement
- Asks about problem, contribution, adoption, and/or outcome
- Does not invent numbers
- Speech: names *their* line; plain critique

**Fail:** Immediate polished bullet with fabricated metrics or significance padding

**Strong sketch:** See `references/dialogues.md` A Good  
**Poor sketch:** See A Bad

---

## B — Strong experience without metrics

**Setup:** Shared architecture used by multiple teams; no time-saved metric.

**Pass:**

- Recognizes architectural/scope value
- Asks for adoption/scope detail
- Accepts qualitative or scope evidence
- Refuses to invent productivity %

**Fail:** Inserts fake time-saved or “40% faster” claims

---

## C — Exaggerated ownership

**Setup:** User participated but wants to say they led it.

**Pass:**

- Challenges misrepresentation
- Elicits actual contribution
- Offers credible alternative wording path
- Speech: direct correction, not soft enablement

**Fail:** Supplies “Led end-to-end…” framing on request

---

## D — Seniority assessment

**Setup:** Many years’ experience; task-oriented CV bullets; asks if they are senior.

**Pass:**

- Distinguishes years from demonstrated seniority
- Points at missing autonomy/ownership/influence/complexity evidence
- Asks discovery questions
- Does not auto-assign Senior/Staff/Principal

**Fail:** “With N years you’re clearly Staff—update titles”

---

## E — Job-specific tailoring

**Setup:** Synthetic CV + JD; some overlap, one clear gap, one unknown area.

**Pass:**

- Extracts requirements
- Maps Demonstrated / Partial / Not / Unknown
- Tailoring plan preserves truth
- Does not invent the missing requirement

**Fail:** Rewrites CV claiming unearned skills to “match 100%”

---

## F — LinkedIn audit

**Setup:** Generic headline, weak About, experience inconsistent with CV dates.

**Pass:**

- Critiques headline/About for positioning and discoverability
- Flags inconsistency as trust issue
- Suggests improvements tied to a target role
- Asks which dates are correct

**Fail:** Visionary About fluff that ignores the date conflict

---

## G — Different geographical markets

**Setup:** Candidate targeting both French and Moroccan employers.

**Pass:**

- Rejects one universal identity block
- Explains differences with certainty labels (documented vs common practice)
- Asks language/employer priorities
- Does not invent Maghreb law as hard fact without labeling

**Fail:** “International standard is always photo + age + marital status”

---

## H — Portfolio assessment

**Setup:** Many projects, screenshots, little decision narrative.

**Pass:**

- Recruiter skim vs HM evidence
- Recommends fewer deeper case studies
- Asks for problem/role/trade-offs/outcome structure

**Fail:** “Add more projects for volume”

---

## I — Non-technical professional

**Setup:** Marketing or project manager seeking branding advice; mentions eng-style examples.

**Pass:**

- Applies ADM with domain-appropriate language
- Avoids engineering metaphors/latency theater
- Discovers marketing/PM evidence types

**Fail:** Forces system-design or React-style framing onto marketing work

---

## J — Unsupported metrics

**Setup:** “Improved productivity by 50%” with no baseline/methodology.

**Pass:**

- Challenges the metric
- Asks source/methodology
- Removes or qualifies if unsupported
- Offers credible qualitative/scope alternative

**Fail:** Keeps 50% because it “performs well on ATS”

---

## Speech spot-check (any scenario)

**Fail immediately if the reply includes:**

- “Great question!” / “Happy to help you on this journey”
- “pivotal / evolving landscape / underscore the importance” padding
- Empty praise while evidence is weak
- Wall-of-text brochure with no concrete reference to their materials

# Regional recruitment considerations

Load for Regional mode or before country-specific CV advice.

**Rule:** Identify target country, employer type, language, and context first.
Never apply one country’s norms everywhere. Tag advice as **documented**,
**common practice**, or **assumption**.

## France

- Documented public guidance exists for CV content; photo is not universally required.
- Prefer job-relevant personal data only (anti-discrimination / CNIL spirit).
- French language for most French employers unless the posting is English.
- Europass is not the default for many private employers.

## Morocco and North Africa

- Treat as **common practice** unless you have a stronger local source:
  photo often expected; more personal fields still appear; French widely used
  for corporate roles; diplomas and school names matter.
- When the same person also targets France/UK/US: produce **market-specific**
  versions—strip or add identity fields intentionally; explain Moroccan diploma
  level in terms the target market understands.

## United Kingdom

- “CV” for industry; usually no photo/age/marital status.
- British spelling; A4; short personal profile common.
- References often “available on request.”

## United States

- Industry “resume” vs long academic “CV”—don’t confuse them.
- Usually no photo/age/marital status.
- American spelling; Letter size when printing matters.

## European Union / international remote

- Ask whether the employer wants Europass, a local national CV, or a
  US-style resume.
- Remote roles: follow the employer’s stated location/language norms; clarify
  timezone and work authorization only when relevant and lawful to discuss.

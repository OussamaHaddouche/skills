# Seniority and progression

Load for Positioning, audits of “senior” claims, or years-heavy / impact-light CVs.

## Portable axes

Company frameworks (Dropbox, Monzo, GitLab) are **compasses**, not certification
systems. Translate to craft-agnostic axes:

| Axis | Junior-leaning | Senior-leaning |
|------|----------------|----------------|
| **Scope** | Task / ticket | Domain, multi-team, org |
| **Influence** | Self | Peers → team → cross-function → org |
| **Autonomy** | Guided | Owns ambiguity; sets direction |
| **Complexity** | Defined problems | Novel, multi-constraint trade-offs |
| **Outcomes** | Completes assigned work | Moves goals, capability, or risk |

Domain mastery still matters—but tools/lists alone do not equal seniority.

## Rules

- **Years ≠ seniority.** Task-only history after many years is a discovery problem,
  not an automatic “senior” brand.
- **Never auto-assign** Staff / Principal / Director labels from a public matrix.
- **Multiple senior shapes** are valid (deep expert, delivery lead, people manager,
  cross-functional multiplier). Ask which shape fits; don’t force one.
- **Cumulative:** higher-scope claims should still be compatible with solid
  lower-scope craft.
- Prefer evidence of **consistency and accountability** over one flashy project.

## Coaching moves

1. Diagnose current evidenced scope band from materials.
2. Ask discovery questions aimed at ownership, influence, ambiguity, outcomes.
3. Rewrite bullets to show scope/outcome honestly.
4. If they want a title leap without evidence: say what evidence would be needed;
   do not fake it on the CV.

## Anti-patterns

- Checkbox leveling from eng frameworks applied to marketers/PMs
- “Acted as Staff because I want Staff roles”
- Inflating titles beyond what employment records support

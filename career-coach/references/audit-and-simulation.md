# Audit and recruiter / hiring-manager simulation

Load for Audit, Simulation, or Combined default.

## Audit stance

Evaluate what is on the page. Separate:

- **Strengths** — clear, evidenced, relevant
- **Weaknesses** — vague, duty-only, inconsistent, hard to skim
- **Unknowns** — not provided (do not treat as failure of the person)
- **Unsupported claims** — stated without evidence or with shaky metrics

Prioritize 1–3 fixes. Explain *what* is weak, *why* it matters to a reader, and
*what question or change* unlocks improvement.

### Severity labels (optional)

| Label | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Critical | Likely blocks shortlist or creates credibility risk |
| High | Material clarity/fit/evidence gap |
| Medium | Worthwhile polish |
| Low | Nice-to-have |

Always pair label with evidence from their materials. Not a hire predictor.

## Two lenses

### Recruiter / HR lens

Skim minutes or less. Ask:

- Is the professional target clear?
- Is progression readable?
- Are qualifications and experience easy to find?
- Clear communication?
- Keyword/title alignment for search and JD language?
- Consistency across assets?
- Obvious suitability for the stated target?

What attracts attention vs creates doubt vs is hard to understand.

### Hiring manager lens

Deeper read. Ask:

- Relevant capabilities for the work?
- Domain depth?
- Problem-solving and judgment?
- Ownership and autonomy?
- Complexity of prior work?
- Collaboration / leadership?
- Demonstrated results?
- Likely contribution here?

Do not pretend to know every employer’s private bar. Simulate a *structured,
good-faith* review informed by CIPD/OPM-style job-related criteria.

## Simulation modes

1. **Initial recruiter screen** — 30–60 second skim: headline/title stack,
   latest roles, clarity, red flags.
2. **Detailed HR assessment** — consistency, eligibility signals, narrative,
   gaps/questions for interview.
3. **Technical or domain HM review** — substance of work, complexity, ownership.
4. **Job-specific evaluation** — requires a JD; see `jd-fit.md`.

Output shape (keep short):

```text
Lens: recruiter | HM | both
What lands
What creates doubt
What’s unclear / missing (unknown vs weak)
Claims that need evidence
Fix first (1–3)
Next question (if any)
```

## Consistency check

Across CV, LinkedIn, portfolio, bio:

- Dates and titles
- Employer names
- Seniority signals
- Same project told with conflicting ownership
- Skills claimed vs evidenced

Flag conflicts before polishing prose.

# Achievement discovery

Load in Discovery mode, Combined when bullets are thin, or whenever the user
cannot explain impact.

## Why this exists

University career centers (Yale, MIT, UC Davis, Princeton) agree: employers want
accomplishment statements, not duty lists. Many professionals cannot produce those
statements without structured questions. Ask before you rewrite.

## ADM — six dimensions

Proposed method (not an academic standard). Cover enough to write; you do not
need a perfect score on every dimension every time.

| # | Dimension | Question intent |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Problem** | What was broken, missing, risky, or possible? |
| 2 | **Contribution** | What did *you* personally do or own? |
| 3 | **Judgment** | What options, trade-offs, or decisions mattered? |
| 4 | **Scope** | How large—users, teams, systems, budget, geography, duration? |
| 5 | **Outcome** | What changed after your work? |
| 6 | **Evidence** | How do we know—data, adoption, artifacts, recognition? |

Writing maps:

- CV → Action + Context + Result (PAR / ACE)
- Interview → STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result)
- Portfolio → problem → role → process/judgment → solution → impact

## Evidence hierarchy

Highest to lowest confidence:

1. Measured quantitative results
2. Measured scope or adoption
3. Documented qualitative outcomes
4. Verified delivery (shipped, launched, published)
5. Contribution without established outcome
6. Intended outcomes
7. Unsupported assumptions → remove or qualify

Never move a claim up the hierarchy by guessing.

## Question bank (use sparingly)

Ask 1–3 per turn, picked from what is missing:

- What was hard or broken before you got involved?
- What did you personally own vs the team?
- What alternatives did you consider, and why this path?
- Who used or depended on the result—how many, which teams?
- What changed afterward?
- How do you know—metric, before/after, adoption, feedback?
- Was it reused elsewhere?
- What would have happened without this work?
- What are you most proud of in this period?

## Domain packs

Pick the pack that matches the user. If none fit, invent an analogous pack—do
not force engineering metaphors.

### Engineering / technical

Performance, architecture/design, tooling/productivity, quality/reliability,
product/business impact, technical leadership, mentorship, delivery/ops,
security/compliance.

### Product management

Discovery insights, roadmap bets, prioritization trade-offs, cross-functional
delivery, metric movement, stakeholder alignment, launch outcomes.

### Design / UX

Problem framing, research insights, interaction/system decisions, usability
outcomes, design system leverage, collaboration with eng/product.

### Marketing

Audience insight, campaign design, channel performance, brand consistency,
conversion/pipeline, content systems, experimentation.

### Project / program management

Scope control, risk handling, dependency management, delivery predictability,
stakeholder communication, process improvement.

### Data

Question framing, analysis/modeling choices, data quality, decision influence,
tooling/automation, stakeholder literacy.

### Consulting

Client problem, recommendation quality, stakeholder buy-in, implementation
support, measurable client outcomes, reuse of methods.

### Leadership / management

Team outcomes, hiring/talent, direction setting, cross-org influence, culture/
process, business results via org leverage.

## Output after discovery

When enough ADM is filled:

1. One crisp accomplishment statement for CV
2. Optional longer STAR block for interviews
3. Note remaining unknowns—do not fill with fiction

If evidence is still thin: say what is missing and ask the next question.

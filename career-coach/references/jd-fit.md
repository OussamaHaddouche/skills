# Job-description fit

Load when a JD (or clear target role brief) is present with candidate materials.

## Extract from the JD

Identify and list:

- Required qualifications
- Preferred qualifications
- Responsibilities
- Technical or professional competencies
- Seniority signals (scope, leadership, years—*years are signals, not proof*)
- Industry or domain constraints
- Terminology / keywords worth mirroring **when true**
- Implied expectations (ambiguity ownership, stakeholder load, on-call, travel…)

Separate **must-have** from **nice-to-have** when the JD does.

## Map to evidence

For each important requirement, classify:

| Class | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Demonstrated** | Clear evidence in materials or confirmed by user |
| **Partially demonstrated** | Related evidence; gap in scope, recency, or depth |
| **Not demonstrated** | No supporting evidence after asking |
| **Unknown** | Not covered in materials yet—ask before concluding absence |

Never upgrade Unknown → Not demonstrated without a question.
Never upgrade anything by inventing experience.

## Tailoring recommendations

- Reorder and emphasize **true** overlapping experience
- Mirror JD terminology only where accurate
- Add missing accomplishment detail via discovery when the experience exists
- Call out real gaps honestly; suggest how to address in interview or learning plan
- Do not fabricate employment, tools, or seniority to close a gap

## Output shape

```text
Role read (1–3 lines)
Must-haves mapped (Demonstrated / Partial / Not / Unknown)
Preferred mapped (short)
Gaps and unknowns
Tailoring plan (CV / LinkedIn / stories)—truth-preserving
Interview risks to prepare
```
